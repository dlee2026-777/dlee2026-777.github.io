Intro

Hello! I'm a Junior at UC Berkeley studying physics and mathematics. My research interests are in the intersection of theoretical condensed matter physics and superconducting qubits. I'm currently conducting independent theoretical work on topological multiterminal Josephson junctions (MTJJ) with the help of Dr. Junyeong Ahn at UT Austin. I'm also working on implementing junction readout to merged-element transmons (MET) in Quantum Devices Group @ Berkeley.
